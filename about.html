About ERS Technologies Ltd

ERS Technologies Ltd is a Nigerian technology company providing intelligent digital solutions for revenue expansion, taxpayer intelligence, compliance monitoring, debt recovery and financial technology services in Africa.

We help governments, revenue authorities, institutions and enterprises discover untapped revenue opportunities, digitize collections, recover outstanding debts, eliminate leakages and make data-driven decisions through cutting-edge technology.
